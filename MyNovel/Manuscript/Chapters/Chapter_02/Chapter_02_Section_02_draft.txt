Maya's hands were shaking against Tony's chest as she struggled to breathe. She had managed to tell him Tiffany was gone, but the words themselves felt like they'd been torn from her throat by some unseen force. Tony held her closer, his warmth enveloping her like a sanctuary.

"Tell me what happened," he whispered, his voice low and reassuring. "What did you see?"

The question stung, forcing Maya back into the chaos of moments before. Her eyes welled with tears as she fought to put the pieces together. "He... he grabbed Tiffany first," she stuttered. "Then he turned toward me. I think I froze for a second, I don't know... and then I hit him from behind."

Tony's grip on her tightened ever so slightly. "From behind? What do you mean?"

Maya's gaze drifted downward, her mind replaying the events in fits and starts. "I was trying to help Tiffany, and... I just reacted." The shame and fear tangled within her, making her words come out in a jumbled mess.

Tony's arms wrapped around her more securely. "It's okay, Maya. You did what you thought was right in that moment. Just keep going."

She took a deep breath, trying to collect herself. "I hit him, and he turned because of it. Then Tiffany scratched his cheek three times – I remember that part clearly because... because it made me feel a little better, I guess."

Tony's expression remained calm, but his voice betrayed a hint of tension when he spoke next. "And then?"

Maya's memories came flooding back now, piecemeal and fragmented. "He hit me across the cheek. It hurt a lot. And then... he threw Tiffany into the van, and drove away with her inside."

The last of Tony's control cracked as he processed her words. His hold on her became more possessive, his chest rising and falling with each slow breath. A soft curse escaped his lips as the full weight of Maya's account sank in.

"Sorry," Maya whispered, feeling guilty for putting him through this.

Tony's grip gentled once more, his fingers intertwining with hers as he brought them up to rest against his heart. "You don't have anything to be sorry about, Maya. We'll find Tiffany. I promise you that."

For a moment, the two simply sat there, hands clasped together amidst the chaos of emotions still swirling around them. The silence was heavy with the weight of what had happened, but at least they were no longer alone in it.
