Maya's breath caught in her chest as she struggled to find the words to tell Tony what had happened. Her voice shook as she spoke, her eyes fixed on his concerned face. "Tony...Tiffany...she's gone."

Tony's expression remained calm, but his grip on Maya tightened slightly. He leaned forward, his eyes scanning hers for any hint of what was happening. "What do you mean? Where's Tiffany?"

Maya's tears began to fall, and she buried her face against Tony's chest. She couldn't bear to look at him while telling him this. "She was walking with me...and then he just grabbed her. I froze, Tony. I didn't know what to do."

Tony's arms wrapped around her, holding her close as he stroked her hair. "It's okay, baby. You're safe. Tell me what happened."

Maya took a deep breath and tried to compose herself. Her mind flooded with images from earlier that afternoon. The unfamiliar man approaching them, Tiffany's panic, and then...the fight. "He came out of nowhere," Maya continued, her voice barely above a whisper. "He grabbed Tiffany, and she fought back. She scratched him, Tony. Three times. She broke the skin and drew blood."

Tony's jaw clenched, but he said nothing. Instead, he continued to hold Maya, offering her comfort as she recounted the events.

Maya's words poured out in a jumbled mess. She remembered the man's attention turning toward her after she struck him from behind. She recalled the pain in her cheek when he hit her, the temporary red mark that still lingered. "I was so scared, Tony. I didn't know what to do. Tiffany was screaming and trying to get away..."

As she spoke, Maya felt the weight of what had happened settle on her shoulders. She had failed to protect Tiffany, and now she was left feeling helpless and terrified for her friend's safety.

Tony remained silent, his face set in a mask of calm determination. But beneath the surface, Maya could sense the anger and concern brewing inside him. He knew it wasn't just about finding Tiffany; it was about making sure she was safe, that justice was served, and the people responsible were held accountable.

"Then what?" Tony asked finally, his voice gentle but firm. "What happened after that?"

Maya nodded, taking a deep breath before continuing. "He threw her into the van...and drove away. I watched them go, Tony. I watched them disappear into traffic."

The words hung in the air like a challenge, a warning sign that something terrible was unfolding. Tony's grip on Maya tightened as he processed the information, his mind racing with thoughts of how to find Tiffany and bring her home safely.

"We'll get her back, Maya," Tony whispered, his voice full of conviction. "We'll find her and make sure she's safe. I promise."
