The unfamiliar man moved into their path on the dusty road between the gas station and home. He didn't seem to have been trailing them for a long time, simply stepping directly into their space where Tiffany and Maya stood talking. The late afternoon sun cast long, uneven shadows, doing little to illuminate the man’s features clearly.

“Excuse me,” he said, his voice unremarkable but too loud in the quiet air, halting their casual exchange.

Tiffany stepped slightly forward, instinctively placing herself marginally ahead of Maya. “Can we help you?”

Maya watched the stranger, her hand resting near her hip, her expression shifting into a careful observation. A slight tension tightened the vanilla-scented air around her as she assessed him, noting the suspicious bulk of the dark van parked just behind him.

The man offered no verbal answer, merely taking another step closer until he was within arm's reach. Before either girl could process what was happening next, he reached out with surprising speed and gripped Tiffany’s upper arm, pulling her toward the open side door of the van.

A startled cry caught in Tiffany’s throat, but she reacted instantly. She twisted hard against his grip, her free elbow driving up sharply into his ribs while simultaneously aiming a sharp punch towards his sternum. The Kidnapper grunted, visibly reacting to the unexpected sting, momentarily loosening his hold on her arm. Seeing the chance, Tiffany slipped her body out from under his grasp and delivered a quick, controlled strike upward into his exposed thigh muscle, followed by another jab aimed at his forearm—clean, targeted blows that elicited small, human noises of surprise and minor discomfort.

Despite these efforts, the man was significantly larger than her, and when he managed to shift his weight, it became clear. With a low grunt that strained the tendons in his neck, he wrapped one heavy arm around her waist and bent his knees, hoisting her straight up off the ground. It was not an effortless lift; the sudden strain made his chest rise visibly, his posture momentarily shifting as he adjusted to the weight of both of them. Tiffany continued to thrash, kicking her legs and batting at his arm, but he maintained the grip, carrying her firmly against his hip as he pivoted toward the dark opening of the van.

“Hey!” Maya suddenly cried out, breaking her frozen state. A surge of adrenaline propelled her forward, and she broke into a run, lunging after them down the roadside. She reached out desperately, throwing a clumsy, flailing right fist that connected with the back of his thigh with a dull *thwack*. The impact did little to slow him, who merely absorbed it with a slight stumble, but it bought nothing.

As the Kidnapper finally maneuvered them inside, turning his body slightly to manage the transfer, Tiffany spotted a sliver of opportunity. As he turned fully toward Maya’s trailing figure, she wrenched herself free from his side grasp enough to reach up. Her fingernails dug deep into the exposed flesh of his left cheek, raking hard enough to break the skin and draw three distinct beads of crimson blood.

In that exact moment, before the scratch could even register fully, the Kidnapper snapped his head toward Maya. With a forceful, open-handed backhand strike across the side of her face, the blow landed squarely on her cheek. Maya gasped—a sharp, inhaled sound—and pitched sideways, falling heavily onto the dry dirt roadside.

With barely a pause, the Kidnapper pivoted and bodily thrust Tiffany through the gaping side door opening of the van. The rough metal frame scraped against her as she tumbled inside. He immediately spun around, pulling the heavy side door shut with a decisive clang, plunging the interior into shadow. He moved efficiently to the wheel, started the engine with a roar, and drove away down the road.

Maya remained crumpled just outside the van’s edge, watching the dark shape disappear around the curve of the road. She stayed fixed in that position, breath held tight in her chest, her vision locked on the retreating taillights. Slowly, irrevocably, her knees buckled beneath her, and she collapsed onto the dust beside the curb.
