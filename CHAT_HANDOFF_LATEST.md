### Future story planning

Future novel premises and unstarted story concepts are maintained separately in the private ChatGPT Library at `/Writing/FUTURE_STORY_PLANNING.md`. This is a parking record, not an active novel, canon source, or software-project plan.

## Documents that are not active handoffs

Superseded audits, redesign plans, repair plans, and dated handoff copies are historical material, not current instructions. Do not recreate them or use them to infer current project status.

## Rules that prevent handoff drift

1. Each writing application has one active technical handoff.
2. Each novel has one active novel handoff.
3. Future story concepts have one separate planning record.
4. Do not duplicate current status across documents. Link to the authoritative record instead.
5. After a meaningful milestone, update the relevant handoff after the milestone finishes, not before. Include the resulting commit and verification status.
6. Clearly label generated prose as pending review until the author accepts it and it is saved in the manuscript.
7. Never delete an archive or overwrite local work as part of handoff maintenance.
8. When sources disagree, record the conflict and pause. Do not choose a version by guessing.
